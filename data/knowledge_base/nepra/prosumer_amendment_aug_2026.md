## Purpose

This notification amends provisions of the National Electric Power Regulatory Authority (Prosumer) Regulations, 2026.

The amendment modifies provisions concerning concurrence and approval for distributed generation.

---

## Distributed Generation up to 25 kW

The amendment provides that distributed generation facilities having a capacity up to 25 kW do not require concurrence.

The concerned licensee shall accord approval in accordance with the applicable regulatory provisions.

---

## Regulatory Interpretation

This amendment is particularly relevant when SolarGrid evaluates small distributed-generation or prosumer projects.

The 25 kW threshold should not be automatically applied to utility-scale renewable-energy projects.

A project assessment must first identify the project's technology, capacity, connection arrangement, and applicable regulatory framework.

---

## Relevance to SolarGrid AI

The Regulatory Intelligence Agent should use this source when:

1. Assessing small distributed-generation projects.
2. Determining whether the project falls within the prosumer/distributed-generation framework.
3. Checking whether concurrence is required based on the applicable capacity threshold.
4. Comparing the current amendment with earlier versions of the Prosumer Regulations.

For a utility-scale solar PV project, the agent should not use the 25 kW distributed-generation provision as the primary regulatory framework.

---

## Evidence Limitation

This amendment addresses specific regulatory provisions concerning prosumer/distributed-generation arrangements.

It does not establish the technical feasibility, grid capacity, financial viability, or connection point of a particular renewable-energy project.

Those questions require additional project-specific information and other applicable regulatory and technical sources.
