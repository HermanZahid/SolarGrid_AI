## Amendment to Regulation 3

In the proviso to sub-regulation (11) of regulation 3, after the word "concurrence", the words "or approval" are inserted.

---

## 25 kW Distributed Generation Provision

In sub-regulation (1) of regulation 4, after the first proviso, a second proviso is inserted.

A prosumer having a distributed generation facility of 25 kW or below capacity shall not be required to seek concurrence from the Authority.

For a distributed generation facility of 25 kW or below capacity, the concerned licensee shall accord its approval.

---

## Amendment to Regulation 4(4)

In sub-regulation (4) of regulation 4:

1. After the first occurrence of the word "concurrence", the words "or approval" are inserted.
2. After the second occurrence of the word "concurrence", the words "or approval as the case may be" are inserted.

---

## Amendment to the Distributed Generation Interconnection Agreement

In clause 4 of the Distributed Generation Interconnection Agreement provided in Schedule-I, after the words "for the facility", the words "if applicable" are inserted.
