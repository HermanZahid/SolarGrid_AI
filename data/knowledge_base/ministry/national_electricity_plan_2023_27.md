## Purpose

The National Electricity Plan 2023–27 provides a national framework for electricity-sector planning and implementation in Pakistan.

It translates the direction of the National Electricity Policy into planning priorities and actions for the electricity sector.

---

## Relevance to Renewable Energy

The National Electricity Plan is relevant to renewable-energy project assessment because renewable-energy development forms part of Pakistan's broader electricity-sector planning framework.

SolarGrid AI should use the Plan as a policy and planning context source rather than treating it as a project-specific technical or financial feasibility document.

---

## Renewable-Energy Project Assessment

For a proposed renewable-energy project, the Plan can provide context for:

1. National electricity-sector planning priorities.
2. Development of generation capacity.
3. Integration of renewable-energy resources into the electricity system.
4. Electricity-sector development and planning considerations.
5. Alignment of a proposed project with broader electricity-sector planning.

---

## Relationship with Other Sources

The National Electricity Plan should be interpreted together with more specific regulatory and technical sources.

For example:

- NEPRA regulations provide regulatory requirements.
- NEPRA grid-connectivity standards provide technical connectivity requirements.
- The Grid Code provides detailed grid-operation and connection requirements.
- National electricity policies provide broader policy direction.
- Project-specific technical and financial studies are required for project-level feasibility.

The National Electricity Plan should therefore not be treated as a substitute for a project-specific feasibility study or grid study.

## SolarGrid AI Usage

The Regulatory Intelligence Agent can use this source when answering questions such as:

- How does a proposed renewable-energy project fit within Pakistan's electricity-sector planning framework?
- What national planning context is relevant to renewable-energy development?
- Which official national planning document should be considered when assessing renewable-energy projects?

When answering a project-specific technical or financial question, the agent should retrieve more specific technical or financial evidence rather than relying solely on this Plan.

---

## Evidence Limitation

This document provides national electricity-sector planning context.

It does not by itself establish:

- the grid capacity available at a particular location;
- approval of a particular project;
- a guaranteed grid connection;
- project-specific electricity generation;
- project-specific CAPEX or OPEX;
- project-specific financial returns.

Such conclusions require additional evidence and project-specific analysis.
