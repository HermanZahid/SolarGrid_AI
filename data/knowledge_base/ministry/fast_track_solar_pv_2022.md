## Purpose

The Framework Guidelines for Fast Track Solar PV Initiatives provide a framework for accelerating the development of solar photovoltaic generation projects in Pakistan.

The framework is relevant to the development and procurement of solar PV generation and forms part of Pakistan's initiatives to increase solar-energy deployment.

---

## Key Solar PV Initiatives

The framework includes initiatives for solar PV generation in different applications and project configurations.

These include:

1. Development of utility-scale solar PV projects.
2. Solar PV generation on selected 11 kV feeders through competitive bidding.
3. Solarization of public-sector buildings.
4. Development of solar PV capacity through defined project-development and procurement mechanisms.

---

## Solar PV on 11 kV Feeders

The framework includes solar PV generation on 11 kV feeders.

The initiative contemplates solar PV projects on selected high-loss feeders, with projects up to a maximum capacity of 4 MW being considered through competitive bidding.

This is relevant to distributed or feeder-level solar development and should not automatically be interpreted as the regulatory framework for every utility-scale solar PV project.

## Relationship With Other Regulatory Sources

This framework provides policy and project-development context.

It does not replace:

- NEPRA regulations;
- grid-connectivity requirements;
- the Grid Code;
- applicable distribution or transmission requirements;
- project-specific grid studies;
- environmental or land approvals; or
- detailed financial feasibility analysis.

SolarGrid AI should therefore retrieve this source together with the relevant technical and regulatory sources when evaluating a proposed solar project.

---

## Evidence Limitation

The framework does not establish that a particular project will receive approval, obtain a specific grid connection, or achieve a particular financial return.

Project-specific conclusions require additional evidence, including technical, grid, financial, regulatory, land, and site-specific information.
